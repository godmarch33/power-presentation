## `sfx-analysis.json` — analysis metadata of Kenney SFX (CC0)

- The JSON is brag's generated analysis (loudness, brightness, high-frequency risk,
  envelope shape, transient sharpness, recommended usage) of 228 sound-effect files.
- The analysed sounds are from **Kenney** (https://kenney.nl/), released under
  **CC0 1.0 Universal**. The `.ogg` files are **not** included in this plugin; they are
  fetched at first run into `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_DATA}/media-packs/` with their own

## `cue-presets/*.music-cues.json` — beat/cue metadata for ende.app tracks (CC BY 4.0)

- Five presets, generated by brag's `analyze_music_cues.py` (now ported to
  `scripts/analyze_music_cues.py`, MIT) from the ende.app **"Happy Beats / Business
  Moves"** series, volumes 1, 9, 10, 11 and 12.
- The music tracks are by **Sascha Ende** (https://ende.app/en), licensed
  **CC BY 4.0** under the ende.app standard licence
  (https://ende.app/en/standard-license). **The `.mp3` files are NOT included** in this
  plugin (no music in the package). Only the derived timing metadata is.
- When a video uses one of these tracks (from the first-run media pack), the delivery
  must carry the credit line in the description/credits and `run-report.json`:

  ```
  Music: "Happy Beats / Business Moves Vol. <N>" by Sascha Ende / ende.app (CC BY 4.0)
  ```

  Content ID registration of videos using these tracks is forbidden by the ende.app
  licence.
- The preset JSON files are covered by brag's MIT licence (notice above).
- Four more presets (stored as compact JSON), generated on 2026-09-27 with the ported
  `scripts/analyze_music_cues.py` (librosa 1.0.0; it reproduces the Vol. 9 preset exactly) from
  the ende.app tracks of the `music-ende-calm` pack: "Documentary Music - The Turning Point",
  "9. Minimalist (Neutral Focus)", "Between Hours" and "Podcast Music Vol. 24 [Morning Light]"
  by **Sascha Ende**, CC BY 4.0 under the same ende.app licence, the same credit line
  (`Music: "<title>" by Sascha Ende / ende.app (CC BY 4.0)`) and the same Content ID ban. The
  `.mp3` files are not included and the plugin does not download them (ende.app needs a free account);
  `media-packs.mjs fetch --pack music-ende-calm --from-dir <dir>` imports them.
